<h1><a href="https://adamfeher3.github.io/classic_tetris_game/">Classic Tetris</a></h1>
<p>
  A classic <b>Tetris-style</b> puzzle game<br/>
  <b>Move, rotate</b> and <b>place</b> falling pieces to complete horizontal lines
</p>
<h2>🎮 Controls</h2>
<h3><b>Key&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Action</b></h3>
<p>
  ← or A&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Move piece left<br/>
  → or D&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Move piece right<br/>
  ↓ or S&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Move piece down<br/>
  ↑ or W&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Rotate piece<br/>
  SPACE&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&nbsp;Hard drop<br/>
  P or ESC&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&nbsp;Pause / Resume
</p>
<h2>🛠 Built With</h2>
<p>
  <b>• HTML</b> -- page structure<br/>
  <b>• CSS</b> -- styling and game interface<br/>
  <b>• JavaScript</b> -- game logic and interactions
</p>
